# Delegate channel folder administration

The creator becomes the first administrator of a channel folder. Folder administrators manage its access and can appoint other administrators, and an organization administrator can also appoint them. A channel move can change the audience for its history, so moves between folders require administration rights on both. Moving to the top level requires administration rights on the source folder, while moving from the top level requires permission to manage the channel and administer the destination folder.
