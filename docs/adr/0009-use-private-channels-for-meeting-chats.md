# Use private channels for meeting chats

A meeting chat is a private channel without topics whose subscribers are the meeting's attendees, shown with direct message conversations rather than in the channel list. A group direct message would look the same, but its members are part of its identity and cannot change, while people are invited to meetings after they are scheduled. A private channel gives the chat Zulip's usual permissions, history, unread counts, notifications and calls, and inviting someone to the meeting subscribes them. The cost is that every place where Zulip lists channels has to leave meeting chats out, and clients that do not know about meeting chats show them as ordinary private channels.
