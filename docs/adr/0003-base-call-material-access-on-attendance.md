# Base access to call recordings and transcripts on attendance

The [call chat](0001-use-zulip-conversations-for-call-chat.md) can include people who missed the call. Each recording or transcript grants default access to the users who were in the call at some point during its capture. The call organizer can explicitly share it with people who were absent. This gives each artifact an audience based on who was present while it was captured, independently of who can read the call chat.
