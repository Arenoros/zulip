## Names of channels in folders

The name of a channel in a folder starts with the name of the folder, like
`Engineering/design`. This lets channels in different folders have the same
name after the name of the folder, like `Engineering/general` and
`Marketing/general`. Neither the name of a folder nor the part of a channel
name after it can contain `/`.

The left sidebar shows a channel in the section of its folder without the name
of the folder. Everywhere else, including search, channel links, and channel
settings, the channel is shown with its full name.

Moving a channel to another folder or renaming a folder renames the channels
involved. Links to these channels and their messages keep working.
[Integrations](/help/integrations-overview) and [bots](/help/bots-overview)
that send messages to a channel by its name need to be updated to use its new
name.

## Configure whether channels are grouped by folder in the Inbox view

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via Inbox view">
    <Steps>
      1. Click the **ellipsis** (<MoreVerticalIcon />)
         to the right of the **Filter** box at the top of the Inbox view.
      1. Click **Group channels by folder** or **Don't group channels by folder**, as
         desired.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via personal settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/preferences" />

      1. Under **Information**, toggle **Group channels by folder in the inbox**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Configure whether channels are grouped by folder in the left sidebar

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via left sidebar">
    <Steps>
      1. Click the **ellipsis** (<MoreVerticalIcon />)
         to the right of the **Filter left sidebar** box in the upper left.
      1. Click **Group channels by folder** or **Don't group channels by folder**, as
         desired.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via personal settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/preferences" />

      1. Under **Left sidebar**, toggle **Group channels by folder in the left
         sidebar**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
