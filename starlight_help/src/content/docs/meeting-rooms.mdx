Users can book your organization's meeting rooms for meetings in the
[calendar](/help/calendar#book-a-meeting-room). A room accepts a meeting
only if no other meeting booked it at that time.

## Add a meeting room

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/meeting-rooms" />

  1. Click **Add meeting room**.
  1. Enter the room's **Name**, and if you like, its **Capacity**, its
     **Location**, such as the building and floor, and its **Exchange
     mailbox**.
  1. Click **Add**.
</FlattenedSteps>

## Edit a meeting room

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/meeting-rooms" />

  1. In the **Actions** column, click the **edit** (<EditIcon />) icon
     for the room you want to edit.
  1. Make the changes you want, and click **Save changes**.
</FlattenedSteps>

## Archive a meeting room

An archived room can no longer be booked, but the meetings that booked
it keep it.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/meeting-rooms" />

  1. In the **Actions** column, click the **archive** (<ArchiveIcon />)
     icon for the room you want to archive.
</FlattenedSteps>

<ZulipTip>
  To make an archived room bookable again, click the **unarchive**
  (<UnarchiveIcon />) icon.
</ZulipTip>
