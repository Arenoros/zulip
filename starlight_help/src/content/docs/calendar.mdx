Zulip's calendar shows your meetings and events a week at a time. It
opens in place of your conversations, and you can go back to the
conversation you were reading without losing your place.

The **calendar** (<CalendarDaysIcon />) icon in the upper right shows
how many meeting invitations you haven't answered yet.

The calendar shows times in the [time zone](/help/change-your-timezone)
of your Zulip profile, even when your computer is set to another one.
Events that last all day, or for a day or more, are shown above the
hours of the day.

      <GoToCalendar />
    </FlattenedSteps>

</Tabs>

## Choose the week to show

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click **Today** to show the current week, or the
         **previous week** (<ChevronLeftIcon />) and **next week**
         (<ChevronRightIcon />) icons next to it. You can also click
         the dates of the week shown to pick a day, or click a day in
         the small calendar on the left.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Show the whole week or the work week

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the name of the current view in the upper right of the
         calendar, and select a view.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The same menu sets the **Time scale**, the length of each row of the
calendar, which is the same on all your devices. Turn off
**Colleagues' calendars side by side** there to hide your colleagues'
calendars without unchecking them.

The work week shows your work days, and the calendar opens at the
start of your work day.

## Create an event or a meeting

An event becomes a meeting when you invite colleagues to it.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click **Create** in the upper right of the calendar, or click
         an empty time in the calendar.

      1. Add a title and the colleagues to invite. Click the date and
         time to change them. You can also drag the event in the day on
         the right to move it, or drag its bottom edge to change how
         long it lasts.

      1. Click **Save**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  Click the **attendee options** (<UserPlusIcon />) icon to invite
  optional attendees, or to hide the list of attendees from the people
  you invite. The menus at the top of the form set how the time shows
  in your calendar, your reminder, a category, and whether the event
  is private.
</ZulipTip>

## Schedule a meeting in a channel

A meeting in a channel is discussed in a topic of the channel named
after the meeting, and everyone subscribed to the channel sees it in
their calendar and can join its call, even without an invitation. The
people you invite get an invitation as for any other meeting.
Notification Bot announces the meeting in its topic, as well as when it
is moved or canceled.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the arrow next to **Create** in the upper right of the
         calendar, and select **Meeting in a channel**.

      1. Choose a channel, and add a title and the colleagues to
         invite.

      1. Click **Save**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The meeting's channel can't be changed, and its topic keeps its name
when the meeting is renamed, so that links to the topic keep working.
In a channel without topics, the meeting is discussed in the channel's
general chat. A meeting in a channel can't be private.

## Find a time for a meeting

The scheduler shows, for a week from the date of the meeting, when
you and the people you invite are busy or outside of their work hours,
which they set in their [calendar settings](#change-your-calendar-settings).
Next to each person are their local day and time at the start of the
meeting, and **Unavailable** if they cannot make it.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Create or change a meeting, and click **Scheduler** next to
         its time.

      1. Drag the selected time, or click another time, to move the
         meeting. You can also add or remove attendees.

      1. Click **Save** to save the meeting, or **Back** to go back to
         its form.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  Click **Find a time** to move the meeting to the next time in your
  work hours when everyone required is free and at work. **Display
  options** shows the whole day instead of your work hours, and changes
  the scale.
</ZulipTip>

## Book a meeting room

A meeting books a room by inviting it. The room accepts the meeting
only if no other meeting booked it at that time; otherwise, the meeting
isn't saved, and you can choose another room or time. Your organization's
administrators [set up the rooms](/help/meeting-rooms).

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Create or change a meeting, and start typing the room's name in
         **Add a place or a room**.

      1. Choose the room. You can add several rooms, and type a place
         after them.

      1. Click **Save**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  The **Rooms** section of the [scheduler](#find-a-time-for-a-meeting)
  shows when rooms are booked, and **Find a time** looks for a time when
  they are free.
</ZulipTip>

## Repeat an event or a meeting

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click **Create** in the upper right of the calendar, or edit
         the event or meeting.

      1. Click **Series** at the top of the form.

      1. Choose how often it repeats: every few days, weeks on some
         days of the week, months, or years. It repeats forever,
         unless you choose the day it repeats **until**.

      1. Click **Save**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  When you invite colleagues, **Suggested times** lists the times of
  the same length in your work hours when the most of them are free,
  at the first occurrences of a series. Click one to choose it.
</ZulipTip>

An event's times are those of your [time
zone](/help/change-your-timezone). To set them in another time zone,
click the time zone next to the time.

## Change a meeting or event

You can change the meetings you organize, and your personal events.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting or event in the calendar.

      1. Click **Edit**. For a meeting or event that repeats, choose
         whether to change this occurrence or the whole series.

      1. Make your changes, and click **Save**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

When you change one occurrence of a series, you can change its title,
time, place, and description. Its attendees and other settings are
those of the whole series. Changing when the whole series takes place
undoes the changes made to its occurrences, after you confirm.

## Answer an invitation

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting in the calendar.

      1. Click **Accept**, **Decline**, or **Tentative**. For a
         meeting that repeats, choose whether to answer for this
         occurrence or for the whole series.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

If the organizer asked for responses, they get a direct message with
your answer. To add a message for them, type it in the **Message
(optional)** field before you answer; to answer without telling them,
uncheck **Notify the organizer**.

<ZulipTip>
  To keep a meeting in your calendar without blocking your time, click
  the **more actions** (<MoreVerticalIcon />) icon, and select **Follow
  meeting**. Select **Delete from my calendar** there to decline a
  meeting and remove it.
</ZulipTip>

## Invitations and reminders

Notification Bot sends you a direct message when someone invites you
to a meeting, and when a meeting you are invited to is moved or
cancelled, or you are removed from it. As an organizer, you get one
when someone answers your invitation. Click **Open in calendar** in
the message to open the meeting.

Before a meeting starts, at the time of your reminder for it, a
reminder shows at the top of Zulip. Click **Join** to join the
meeting's call, or **Close** to dismiss the reminder.

<ZulipTip>
  Reminders show only while Zulip is open in your browser or in the
  desktop app.
</ZulipTip>

## Open a meeting

You can open a meeting on the whole window to read its description and
see how its attendees responded.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting in the calendar.

      1. Click the **open the meeting** (<MaximizeDiagonalIcon />) icon.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

**Attendee responses** on the right lists the organizer and the
attendees by their response to the meeting, or to this occurrence of a
series. If the organizer hid the list of attendees, only they see it.

From there you can also:

* Answer the invitation, and choose how the meeting shows your time,
  your reminder, and a category.
* Invite more people with the **forward** (<ForwardMessageIcon />)
  icon, if the organizer allows it. The organizer sees who invited
  them.
* Make a new event with the same details with the **copy**
  (<CopyIcon />) icon.
* Open the next occurrences of a series with **All occurrences**, or
  the series itself with **The whole series**.

When you open a meeting you organize, you can change it, and click
**Attendee responses** to see the responses next to it.

## Discuss a meeting

Each meeting has its own chat with everyone invited to it, which is
shown among your direct message conversations, including in your
inbox and in recent conversations, where **Include DMs** shows or
hides it. People invited later join the chat and see its history;
people removed from the meeting stay in it. A series has one chat for
all of its occurrences.

A [meeting in a channel](#schedule-a-meeting-in-a-channel) is
discussed in its topic of the channel instead. People invited to it
who can't access the channel only join its call.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting in the calendar.

      1. Click **Discussion**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The chat is named after the meeting, and its header shows when the
meeting next takes place. To leave the chat, click the **more actions**
(<MoreVerticalIcon />) icon next to it in the left sidebar, and select
**Leave chat**. **Discussion** in the meeting brings you back.

## Join a meeting's call

A meeting with a Zulip call has its call in the meeting's chat. The
first person to join starts it, which posts its link in the chat, and
the people who join after them join the same call. Anyone in the
meeting's chat can join it.

The call of a meeting in a channel takes place in its topic, and anyone
who can access the channel can join it. People invited to the meeting
who can't access the channel can join the call once someone in the
channel has started it.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting in the calendar.

      1. Click **Join**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  To join without your camera, click the arrow next to **Join**, and
  select **Join without video**.
</ZulipTip>

You can also join from the header of the meeting's chat, or with the
**Join the call** link under **Zulip call** in the opened meeting. The
link stays the same for the whole meeting, so you can share it with
the people invited. When the call has not started, the link offers to
start it.

Zulip calls need LiveKit to be your organization's [call
provider](/help/configure-call-provider).

## Meet now

**Meet now** schedules a 30-minute meeting starting now, with a Zulip
call, and joins its call.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click **Meet now** (<VideoCallIcon />) in the upper right of the
         calendar.

      1. Change the title if you like, and add the colleagues to invite.

      1. Click **Join now**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Cancel a meeting or delete an event

You can cancel the meetings you organize, and delete your personal
events. For a meeting or event that repeats, you can cancel one
occurrence or the whole series.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the meeting or event in the calendar.

      1. Click **Cancel meeting** or **Delete**, and confirm.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Show your colleagues' calendars

You can show the calendars of colleagues next to your own, to see when
they are busy. You see the titles only of the meetings you are invited
to, and never of a colleague's private events. Your list of colleagues
is the same on all your devices.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click <CalendarPlusIcon /> **Add calendar** in the panel on the
         left of the calendar.

      1. Select a colleague.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  Use the checkboxes under **Colleagues' calendars** to choose whose
  calendars to show. Click the **remove from the list**
  (<CloseIcon />) icon next to a colleague's name to remove them from
  the list.
</ZulipTip>

## Filter your events

You can hide some kinds of your events, such as personal events,
meetings you haven't answered, events of a category, or recurring
events. **Meetings in channels** shows or hides the meetings in your
channels that you aren't invited to. The filter applies to your own
calendar on this device.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click **Filter** in the upper right of the calendar.

      1. Uncheck the kinds of events to hide. Hover over an item with
         an arrow to choose among its kinds.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  The **Filter** button is highlighted while some events are hidden.
  Click **Reset filters** in its menu to show all your events again.
</ZulipTip>

## Hide the calendar panel

The panel on the left of the calendar can be hidden to make more room
for your week.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <GoToCalendar />

      1. Click the **hide calendar panel** (<PanelLeftIcon />) icon in
         the upper left of the calendar. Click it again to show the
         panel.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Change your calendar settings

Your work days are the days the work week shows, and your work hours
are when suggested meeting times fall. You can also choose the view
the calendar opens in, its time scale, and the reminder for meetings
you are invited to.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/calendar" />

      1. Change the settings you like. They are saved right away.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipTip>
  You can also open these settings from the view menu in the calendar,
  and the view or time scale you choose there becomes your default.
</ZulipTip>
