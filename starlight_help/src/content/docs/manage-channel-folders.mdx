## Create a new channel folder

Everyone other than [guests](/help/guest-users) can create channel folders. You
become the administrator of each folder you create, and can appoint other
administrators.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via channel settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="relative/channel/all" />

      1. Select any channel.

      <SelectChannelViewGeneral />

      1. Under **Settings**, click the **Create new folder** button to the right
         of the folder selection dropdown.
      1. Fill out the requested information, and click **Create**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via organization settings">
    <AdminOnly />

    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/channel-folder-settings" />

      1. Click **Add a new channel folder**.
      1. Fill out channel folder information as desired, and click **Create**.
      1. In the **Name** column, click and drag the vertical dots to reorder channel
         folders.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Manage a channel folder

The administrators of a channel folder can change its name and description,
choose [who can see it](#make-a-channel-folder-private), and choose who else can
administer it. Organization administrators can manage
every channel folder.

Renaming a channel folder renames the channels in it, since the name of a
channel [starts with the name of its folder](/help/channel-folders#names-of-channels-in-folders).

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via left sidebar">
    <Steps>
      1. Hover over the name of a channel folder in the left sidebar.
      1. Click on the **ellipsis** (<MoreVerticalIcon />), and
         click **Manage folder**.
      1. Edit channel folder information as desired, and click **Save changes**.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via channel settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="relative/channel/all" />

      1. Select any channel.

      <SelectChannelViewGeneral />

      1. Under **Settings**, click the **manage folder** (<FolderCogIcon />) icon
         for the channel folder you want to edit in the **Channel folder** dropdown.
      1. Edit channel folder information as desired, and click **Save changes**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via organization settings">
    <AdminOnly />

    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/channel-folder-settings" />

      1. In the **Actions** column, click the **manage folder** (<FolderCogIcon />)
         icon for the channel folder you want to manage.
      1. Edit channel folder information as desired, and click **Save changes**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Make a channel folder private

By default, everyone in the organization can see a channel folder. You can
make a folder private when you create it, or later, by choosing **Who can see
this folder**. The administrators of the folder and organization administrators
can always see it.

In a private folder:

* Only those who can see the folder can see its channels and their messages,
  search them, and subscribe to them. Each channel also keeps its own
  permissions; for example, a private channel in a private folder needs access
  to both.
* Users who can no longer see the folder, for example after they are removed
  from the group that can see it, are unsubscribed from its channels and can
  no longer read their messages. If they get access again, they can
  subscribe again.
* Moving a channel into the folder unsubscribes the subscribers who cannot see
  the folder.
* [Web-public channels](/help/public-access-option) and
  [default channels](/help/set-default-channels-for-new-users) can't be in the
  folder.

Files uploaded in messages in public channels remain accessible to anyone
in the organization who has a link to them, even if the channel is in a
private folder.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via left sidebar">
    <Steps>
      1. Hover over the name of a channel folder in the left sidebar.
      1. Click on the **ellipsis** (<MoreVerticalIcon />), and
         click **Manage folder**.
      1. Under **Who can see this folder**, add the users and groups who
         should see it, and remove **Everyone**.
      1. Click **Save changes**.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Delete a channel folder

The administrators of a channel folder and organization administrators can
delete it.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via channel settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="relative/channel/all" />

      1. Select any channel.

      <SelectChannelViewGeneral />

      1. Under **Settings**, click the **delete** (<TrashIcon />) icon for the
         channel folder you want to delete in the **Channel folder** dropdown.
      1. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via organization settings">
    <AdminOnly />

    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/channel-folder-settings" />

      1. In the **Actions** column, click the **delete** (<TrashIcon />) icon
         for the channel folder you want to delete.
      1. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
