Zulip makes it convenient to add a video or voice call link to any message,
using the call provider (Jitsi, Zoom, etc.)
[configured](/help/configure-call-provider) by your organization's
administrators.

## Start a video call

<ZulipNote>
  When you join a call, you may need to log in to a separate account for the
  call provider (Jitsi, Zoom, etc.).
</ZulipNote>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <StartComposing />

      1. Click the **add video call** (<VideoCallIcon />) icon at the bottom of
         the compose box. This will insert a **Join video call.** link into your
         message.
      1. Send the message.
      1. Click on the link in the message to start or join the call.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    <ZulipTip>
      You can replace the "Join video call." label for the link with any text you
      like.
    </ZulipTip>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Mobile">
    Access this feature by following the web app instructions in your
    mobile device browser.

    Implementation of this feature in the mobile app is tracked [on
    GitHub](https://github.com/zulip/zulip-flutter/issues/1000). If
    you're interested in this feature, please react to the issue's
    description with 👍.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Control a shared screen

When your organization uses LiveKit for calls, call participants can ask to
control a screen shared from the [Zulip desktop app](/apps/) for Windows, for
example to help with a problem. The person sharing the screen decides whether
to give control, and can take it back at any time. Only one participant can
control a screen at a time.

### Request control of a shared screen

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <Steps>
      1. Click **Request control** below the shared screen.
      1. Wait for the person sharing the screen to allow control.
      1. Move your mouse over the shared screen to move their pointer. You can
         click, drag, and scroll with your mouse wheel.
      1. To type on their screen, click it. Typing uses their keyboard layout.
         To use your keyboard on your own computer again, click outside the
         shared screen, or switch to another window.
      1. When you're done, click **Stop controlling** to give back control.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<ZulipNote>
  Some keyboard shortcuts always stay on your own computer, such as
  <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> +
  <kbd>Delete</kbd>, shortcuts with the <kbd>Windows</kbd> key, and your
  browser's shortcuts for tabs and windows.
</ZulipNote>

### Let someone control your screen

<ZulipNote>
  You can only give control of a screen you share from the Zulip desktop app
  for Windows. Windows does not allow control of apps running as administrator
  or of permission prompts. If one of them is the active window when the other
  person uses their mouse or keyboard, control ends, and they can request it
  again.
</ZulipNote>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop">
    <Steps>
      1. Click on the link to the call in the desktop app.
      1. Click **Share screen**, and choose the screen to share.
      1. When someone requests control, click **Allow** in the dialog that
         appears.
      1. To take back control, click **Stop control** at the top of your
         screen.
    </Steps>

    <KeyboardTip>
      You can also use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> +
      <kbd>S</kbd> to take back control.
    </KeyboardTip>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Unlink your Zoom account from Zulip

If you linked your Zoom account to Zulip, and no longer want it to be connected,
you can unlink it.

<Steps>
  1. Log in to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us/), and
     select **Manage**.
  1. Select **Added Apps** and click the **Remove** button next to the Zulip app.
  1. Click **Confirm**.
</Steps>
