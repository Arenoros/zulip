The **conversations view** shows a channel as a list of cards, one for each
[topic](/help/introduction-to-topics). A card shows the topic's first message
and its latest replies, and the most recently active conversation is at the
bottom, right above the button for starting a new one. It's a great way to
follow a channel where several conversations happen at once.

Clicking on a channel in the [left sidebar](/help/left-sidebar) takes you to
its conversations view, unless you have
[configured](#configure-where-channel-links-in-the-left-sidebar-go) channel
links to go elsewhere. Channels where messages can only be sent to the
[general chat](/help/general-chat-topic) topic open in the [channel
feed](/help/channel-feed) instead.

## Read conversations

Messages in cards work like everywhere else in Zulip: you can
[react](/help/emoji-reactions) to them, [edit](/help/edit-a-message) and
[star](/help/star-a-message) them, and they are [marked as
read](/help/marking-messages-as-read) when you see them. A card with unread
messages shows how many there are next to its title.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <Steps>
      1. Click on a channel in the left sidebar.
      1. Scroll up to see conversations that were active earlier.
      1. To see a conversation's earlier replies, click the line above its
         latest replies that says how many there are. Click **Collapse
         replies** to hide them again.
    </Steps>

    <KeyboardTip>
      Use <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> to move between messages, from one
      card to the next.
    </KeyboardTip>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

If there are cards with unread messages above the ones you are looking at, a
button at the top of the view takes you to them.

## Open a conversation

Opening a conversation shows the whole topic: its first message, how many
replies it has, and every reply. The buttons at the top let you
[resolve](/help/resolve-a-topic) or [rename](/help/rename-a-topic) the topic,
configure [notifications](/help/follow-a-topic) for it, copy a link to it, and
use the topic's menu.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <Steps>
      1. Click on the title of a card.
      1. Click the **back** (<kbd>←</kbd>) button in the top left to return to
         the conversation's card.
    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Reply to a conversation

Your reply moves the conversation's card to the bottom of the view, and you stay
in the conversations view.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <Steps>
      1. Click **Reply in topic** at the bottom of a card.
      1. Write your reply, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click the **Send**
         button.
    </Steps>

    <KeyboardTip>
      Use <kbd>R</kbd> to reply to the conversation of the selected message.
    </KeyboardTip>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Start a new conversation

A new conversation always needs a topic.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <Steps>
      1. Click **New conversation** at the bottom of the view.
      1. Enter a topic and your message.
      1. Click **Publish**. The new conversation's card appears at the bottom of
         the view.
    </Steps>

    <KeyboardTip>
      Use <kbd>C</kbd> to start a new conversation.
    </KeyboardTip>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Click **Cancel** to close the form; what you wrote is saved as a
[draft](/help/view-and-edit-your-message-drafts).

## Topic actions on a card

Hover over a card to see buttons for adding an [emoji
reaction](/help/emoji-reactions) to the topic's first message, [resolving the
topic](/help/resolve-a-topic), configuring [notifications](/help/follow-a-topic)
for it, copying a link to it, and opening the topic's menu.
