The **Combined feed** view is a feed of all the unmuted messages you have
received, which combines channel messages and direct messages. It's a great way
to see new messages as they come in.

Consecutive messages from the same conversation are shown together in a card.
The card's header names the channel and topic, or the people in the direct
message conversation, with an **Open conversation** or **Open chat** link to
see the whole conversation. When a card starts on a new day, the date is shown
above it. Other views that combine messages from several conversations, such as
[mentions](/help/view-your-mentions), [starred
messages](/help/star-a-message), and [search
results](/help/search-for-messages), show messages the same way.

You can configure **Combined feed** to be the [home
view](/help/configure-home-view) for the Zulip web app.
