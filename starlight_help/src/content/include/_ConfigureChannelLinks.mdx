You can configure whether channel links in the [left
sidebar](/help/left-sidebar) go to the channel's
[conversations](/help/conversations-view), shown as one card per topic with its
first message and latest replies, the channel feed, a list of topics in the
channel, the top topic, or the top unread topic. By default, they go to the
channel's conversations.
