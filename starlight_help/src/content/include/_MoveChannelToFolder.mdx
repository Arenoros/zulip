To move a channel that is in a folder, you need to administer that folder. To
move a channel into a folder, you need to administer the folder, and also the
channel if it is not in a folder yet. Organization administrators can move
every channel.

Moving a channel changes its name, which
[starts with the name of its folder](/help/channel-folders#names-of-channels-in-folders).
