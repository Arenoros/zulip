# LiveKit calls

This glossary covers calls in Zulip and the experience of joining and participating in them.

## Language

**Call**:
A live conversation where Zulip users can exchange audio, video, or a screen share.

**Call invitation**:
A request for a Zulip user to join a specific call.

**Call participant**:
A person connected to the call.

**Call origin**:
The direct message conversation or channel topic where a call started.

**Call chat**:
The Zulip conversation shown alongside a call. For a direct message call, it follows the people included in the conversation. For a channel call, it is the originating topic.

**Call chat recipient**:
The caller, an original recipient of a direct message call, or an invitee who has joined it. A recipient remains part of the conversation after leaving the call.

**Link-only call**:
A call opened from a standalone call link that carries no association with a Zulip conversation.

**Screen share**:
A live view of a source selected by a call participant and made visible to others in the call.

**Remote control**:
Permission granted by the person sharing a screen for another call participant to operate the shared desktop using a mouse or keyboard.
