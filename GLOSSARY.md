# Zulip sidebar organization

This glossary covers shared channel organization and the personal organization of conversations and contacts.

## Language

**Channel folder**

A named collection of channels shared within a Zulip organization.

**Public channel folder**

A channel folder open to members of the organization.

**Private channel folder**

A channel folder with access limited to explicitly authorized users and groups.

**Channel folder administrator**

A user authorized to manage access and appoint other administrators for a specific channel folder. Their role authorizes the folder's side of a channel move.

**Qualified channel name**

The organization-wide name of a channel in a folder, written as `<folder>/<channel>`. The channel name within the folder is its short name.

**Personal conversation section**

A section of Direct messages whose conversations are selected by the user who owns the section.

**Personal contact section**

A section of the user list whose contacts are selected by the user who owns the section. Its membership is independent of personal conversation sections.

# Chat layout

This glossary covers how messages are presented in a conversation.

## Language

**Bubble layout**

A presentation of a single one-on-one or group direct message conversation in which each message appears as a bubble, with own messages on the right and other people's messages on the left.

**Classic layout**

The presentation of messages that Zulip uses wherever the bubble layout does not apply.

**Message series**

Consecutive messages from one sender in one conversation, each sent within five minutes of the previous one. A change of date or the start of unread messages ends a series.

**Own message**

A message sent by the user who is viewing it.

**Initials avatar**

A default profile picture showing two letters of the user's name on a colored circle.
